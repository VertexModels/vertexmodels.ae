THE STUDIO

One idea.
Many disciplines.

Vertex Models is a Dubai-based model making and fabrication studio working across architectural scale models, 3D modeling and design, digital fabrication and bespoke commissions.

Some projects begin with a complete drawing set. Others start with a sketch or a conversation. Our role is to connect the design decisions with the practical work of making, finishing and presenting the piece.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Purpose before process.

The audience, setting and intended use guide the scale, level of detail and way a piece is made.

02 / RESOLVE

Think through the details.

Geometry, materials, assembly and presentation are considered together, before they become physical constraints.

03 / MAKE

Bring the right people together.

We coordinate design and delivery with specialist production partners, matching the expertise to the commission.

A COLLABORATIVE WAY OF WORKING

Specialist skills.
A considered whole.

We work through strategic partnerships with registered specialist companies. Each project brings together the design, fabrication and delivery expertise its scope requires.

The quotation sets out the contracting supplier, responsibilities and deliverables before work begins.
